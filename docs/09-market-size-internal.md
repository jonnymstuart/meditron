# Internal note: how big is the Swiss ultrasound market, and where do buyers actually look?

Date 2026-10-05. For Rise only, not for the client. Written because "samsung ultraschall" has 30 searches a month in Switzerland and the question is whether search is the right lever at all.

## 1. Who can buy an ultrasound system in Switzerland

| Buyer group | Count | Source | Ultrasound relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physicians in practice (ambulatory) | 22,777 | FMH Ärztestatistik 2024 | the addressable base |
| General internal medicine, ambulatory | 8,654 | FMH 2024 | roughly a third to a half own a system (POCUS and abdominal); growing with SGUM certificates |
| Gynaecology and obstetrics, ambulatory | 2,064 | FMH 2024 | nearly every practice has one; HERA Z20 / V8 territory, highest ticket |
| Radiology, ambulatory | 1,083 | FMH 2024 | institutes, several systems each, tender-driven |

## 2. How many systems change hands per year (estimate)

- Installed base, back of envelope: 6,000–8,000 systems in practices plus 3,000–5,000 in hospitals, so 10,000–13,000 in total.
- Replacement cycle 8–10 years, plus new practices and POCUS adoption: **roughly 1,000–1,500 systems a year nationally, of which 700–900 in the ambulatory sector.**
- Value: Europe's ultrasound market is about USD 2.6–3.1 bn a year (Mordor, Grand View, both 2025/26). Switzerland is about 1.1% of European population with above-average device spend, so **CHF 40–60 m a year at street prices**, of which the practice segment is roughly half. (Ignore the Data Bridge "151 billion" figure that appears in search results; it is a units error.)
- Meditron: 1,000+ Samsung systems installed since 2012, so **75–100 a year, roughly 8–12% of the ambulatory market by units**. Competitors by presence in Swiss search and AI answers: SMD Medical (also Samsung, plus Mindray), Lysis (GE), Art Medical Suisse (used), MHZ, Codeo, plus the manufacturers' direct teams (GE, Philips, Canon, Siemens, Esaote, Mindray).

So the prize is small in buyers and large per buyer: a few hundred decisions a year, each worth CHF 30,000–150,000 with 8–10 years of service attached. One extra gynaecology sale a month pays for this programme several times over.

## 3. Why the search numbers look low, and what they hide

- A Swiss doctor buys a system every 8–10 years. Nobody searches "Samsung Ultraschall" on a Tuesday for fun. 30 searches a month at that purchase value is a serious signal, not a weak one.
- The real search demand sits one step earlier and brand-free: "Ultraschallgerät kaufen" (140/month), "Ultraschallgerät Praxis", "Ultraschallgerät Occasion", "échographe cabinet médical", plus the model names ("HERA Z20", "V8"). Meditron does not rank for the two biggest German ones yet.
- The AI engines are now the first stop for exactly this kind of research question. Meditron already owns "who sells Samsung in Switzerland" in every language; it is absent on price, model comparison and the brand-free German question. That is where the next three months of content go.

## 4. Where Swiss buyers actually form the shortlist

Ranked by how much they move a purchase, with what Meditron could do in each:

1. **The applications specialist and the demo in the practice.** The decisive step. Everything below exists to get that appointment. Make "book a demo in your practice" the one call to action everywhere.
2. **Peer recommendation inside the specialty.** Gynaecologists ask gynaecologists. Levers: named reference practices per specialty on the site, two or three short case studies with a photo and a quote, a "practices near you" line. Cheap, and it feeds AI answers too.
3. **SGUM.** 4,000 members, the Fähigkeitsausweis courses, the annual congress (Davos, 25–27 June 2026), regional sections. Being the equipment partner of a course or a section gets a system into the hands of 30 doctors who are about to buy. Ask Henri what Meditron already does here; it is probably the highest-return channel in the country.
4. **Practice-founder channel.** Every new practice buys a system. FMH Services, the cantonal medical societies, practice-planning firms, Praxissoftware vendors (Vitodata, Axonlab, Ärztekasse) and leasing partners all meet founders months before purchase. Referral agreements and one joint "Praxis eröffnen" guide are worth more than any ad.
5. **Trade fairs and congresses.** KHM congress for GPs (Lucerne, 24–26 June 2026), IFAS (Zurich, 20–22 Oct 2026, ~250 exhibitors, hospital buyers), the specialty congresses (SGGG for gynaecology, SGR for radiology). A booth is CHF 10–30k; a sponsored workshop with a live system is often cheaper and better.
6. **Google and AI assistants.** This programme. Low volume, high intent, and the only channel that works 24/7 in four languages. Realistic share of total leads: 10–20%, but the cheapest leads of all once the pages exist. Keep it, and point it at the brand-free terms.
7. **Hospital tenders.** simap.ch publishes them; Samsung wins some. Needs a bid team, not marketing. Monitor only.
8. **Pre-owned and leasing.** A visible certified pre-owned list and a leasing calculator capture the price-sensitive third of buyers who otherwise go to foreign marketplaces (bimedis, medizinoccasionen). Already on the discussion list.
9. **Paid search.** Google Ads on "Ultraschallgerät kaufen" and the model names in DE and FR: maybe CHF 500–800 a month for 30–50 clicks from genuine buyers. Worth a three-month test once the pages rank, not before.
10. **LinkedIn and social.** Doctors are there but do not buy equipment there. Use it for the people side (team, training, events), not for lead generation.

## 5. What this means for the programme

- Keep the SEO and GEO work exactly as planned; it is cheap and it compounds. Shift the next articles toward the brand-free German buying terms and the model comparisons.
- Add two things that are not SEO and cost almost nothing: three reference-practice case studies, and a one-line "book a demo in your practice" everywhere.
- Raise with Henri in the next meeting: what Meditron does with SGUM, with practice founders, and at KHM and IFAS. If the answer is "little", that is a bigger lever than search, and the agency can run it as a second workstream.
- Measure success in demo requests per month, not in rankings. Ask for the contact-form and phone-lead count from September as the baseline.
