# SEO audit, 2 November 2026: the highest-value changes

Scope: www.meditron.ch, four locales, Samsung ultrasound focus. Evidence from live fetches of eight key pages on 2 Nov, DataForSEO (Lighthouse mobile, Backlinks, Labs ranked keywords for meditron.ch, smdmedical.ch, mhzsa.ch), Search Console screenshots from 23 Oct (401 indexed, 28.3K not indexed), and the weekly DataForSEO rank and AI-prompt measurements. Ranked by expected effect on Swiss rankings and AI answers per hour of work.

## Also found, lower value

- **Letter-spaced headings.** The home H1 and several H2s on every locale are animated letter by letter, so the HTML holds one element per letter; Google reads "T r a i t e z a v e c p r é c i s i o n" or one run-together word. Replace with plain text (found during the FR/IT language audit, docs/15).
- **Trial font in production.** Every page loads "Denim INK WD-TRIAL" in 12 styles. Not an SEO item, but a licence question for Meditron before any redesign work.
- **Duplicate H1 in the DOM.** Every page renders its H1 twice (desktop and phone variants both in the HTML). Google copes, but one H1 per page is cleaner; the fix is a responsive text node instead of two.
- **Backlinks.** meditron.ch: 108 referring domains, DataForSEO rank 198; SMD Medical: 110 domains, rank 257, with 1,933 links; MHZ: 44 domains; Art Medical Suisse: 47. Meditron's real links are good (SGUM, Swiss Sonodays, SGR, BBS Congress, Davos Congress, PSI), but about 30 domains since August are spam (URL shorteners, casino, random blogs). No action needed beyond monitoring; Google ignores them. The playbook's congress and association route is working and should continue.
- **Organization schema** has no `sameAs` (LinkedIn, Samsung partner page) and the two LocalBusiness entries have no geo coordinates or opening hours. Thirty minutes in the custom code.
- **Product schema** is fine on pricing: the Offer carries "Price on request" in CHF and no amount. The whole JSON-LD block is emitted twice per page (desktop and phone variants); harmless, but one copy would do.
- **Italian article URLs for articles 1–6** still English (already in the to-do list).
- **Framer sitemap bug**: six German article URLs in the English sitemap, 404 (already in the to-do list; report to Framer).

## What the data says about where we stand

- **Production is live.** The Z20, V8, R20, V7 copy, the seven probe pages and their links are on www.meditron.ch as of this check. The November measurement is the first one with the new pages in Google's index.
- **Labs history is empty.** DataForSEO's monthly database shows meditron.ch ranking for 2 German, 1 French and 1 Italian keyword, because the site is seven weeks old to Google. The weekly live measurement remains the source of truth; the dashboard is right to use it.
- **Indexing** is the ceiling on everything: 401 pages indexed, the rest waiting behind the old site's crawl load (item 2).
