# Language audit and rule set: French and Italian (with the shared rules for German)

Google Doc for review: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-5KmFZacKNMz2MzmxM4ejfn8HiijZ2nfuMf33PFn_Ek/edit

Date: 2 November 2026. Scope: www.meditron.ch /fr/ and /it/, read on the live site: home, ultrasound hub, HERA Z20, V8, R20, V7, about, team, contact, product and news listings. German was audited separately on 25 October (docs/11, Google Doc for Jean); its rules are folded into the shared set below so all three languages follow one standard.

Purpose: a rule set the team can apply when writing or reviewing any page, plus the current passages that break the rules, with a suggested rewrite. Nothing has been changed on the site. The client's French and Italian speakers decide; approved rules go into the localisation prompt and the glossary (docs/glossary.md).

## 1. What the French and Italian have in common with the German

The three languages share the same root problem: the product and hub copy is a word-for-word rendering of English marketing text. The same ten patterns appear in all three, so the same ten rules fix them.

| # | Rule | Instead of (FR / IT / DE) | Write (FR / IT / DE) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Say what the machine does, not what it is like.** Cut "advanced, innovative, exceptional, sophisticated, excellence, high-performance". | de pointe, avancé, sophistiqué, excellence clinique / all'avanguardia, avanzato, eccezionale, eccellenza clinica / fortschrittlich, innovativ, Exzellenz | name the function: "mesure la raideur du foie en temps réel" / "misura la rigidità del fegato in tempo reale" / "misst die Lebersteifigkeit in Echtzeit" | A Swiss doctor reads adjectives as noise and functions as information. |
| 2 | **Gynaecology and obstetrics, not "women's health".** | santé des femmes, santé de la femme / salute della donna / Frauengesundheit | gynécologie-obstétrique / ginecologia e ostetricia / Gynäkologie und Geburtshilfe | "Women's health" is an English marketing category with no clinical equivalent; it appears on every Z20 page in all three languages. |
| 3 | **Doctors, not "clinicians" or "healthcare providers".** | cliniciens, prestataires de soins, professionnels de santé / clinici, operatori sanitari, professionisti sanitari / Kliniker, Gesundheitsdienstleister | médecins, cabinets, hôpitaux / medici, studi medici, ospedali / Ärztinnen und Ärzte, Praxen, Spitäler | Say who the reader is. |
| 4 | **No "workflow".** | flux de travail / flusso di lavoro / Workflow, Arbeitsabläufe | l'examen, le quotidien, gain de temps / l'esame, la routine, meno tempo / die Untersuchung, der Praxisalltag | "Workflow" exists in all three languages but reads as software jargon in a medical context. |
| 5 | **One word for "quote", the Swiss one.** | devis (FR-France) / preventivo (IT-Italy) / Angebot (DE-Germany) | offre / offerta / Offerte | The site mixes them: FR contact says "devis", FR hub "devis personnalisés", FR meta "offre"; IT contact "preventivo", IT hub "offerta". |
| 6 | **"Assistance", not "support".** | support, support local, support après-vente / supporto, supporto post-vendita / Support | assistance, service après-vente, service technique / assistenza, servizio tecnico / Service, Kundendienst | Anglicism in all three. |
| 7 | **Swiss institutions by their Swiss names.** | assurance maladie, Sécurité sociale / assicurazione sanitaria, ASL / Krankenkasse is fine | caisse-maladie, attestation SSUM / cassa malati, attestato SSUM / Krankenkasse, SGUM-Fähigkeitsausweis | Articles 7–9 already do this; the older pages do not mention them at all, which is a missed trust signal. |
| 8 | **One article per model, kept consistent.** | FR: "L'HERA Z20 … Conçu", "le R20", "Le Samsung V8" / IT: "HERA Z20 definisce" (no article), "Il Samsung V8", "l'R20" / DE: "Das HERA Z20" and "Der HERA Z20" mixed | FR: le HERA Z20, le V8, le R20 (no elision, it is a brand name) / IT: l'HERA Z20, il V8, l'R20 / DE: der HERA Z20, der V8, der R20 | Mixed gender reads as machine translation. |
| 9 | **Numbers and units the Swiss way.** | 1 000 / 1.000 | FR: 1 000 or 1'000 (pick one, the site uses 1 000); IT: 1'000; DE: 1'000; always CHF, never €; 23,8" in FR and IT, 23,8 Zoll in DE | Mixed formats inside one page undermine trust in the numbers. |
| 10 | **Facts about Meditron in every "Why Meditron" block.** | "Meditron garantit l'accès…", "Meditron garantisce l'accesso…", "Meditron gewährleistet Zugang…" | since 2012, over 1'000 systems installed, Rolle and Frauenfeld, demo in your practice, training at go-live and after a few weeks, service in your language | The current blocks are translation clichés ("ensures access", "from consultation to implementation") and say nothing checkable. |

Two site-wide defects that are not language but affect every language:
- **Letter-spaced headings.** The home H1 ("Traitez avec précision" / "Trattare con precisione") and several H2s are animated letter by letter, so the HTML contains one element per letter. Google reads either "T r a i t e z a v e c p r é c i s i o n" or one run-together word. Replace with plain text on all four locales (already on the discuss list as "About page text animation"; it is on the home page too).
- **English fallback.** On FR and IT product pages the highlights, spec rows 5–10, the four new FAQs and the seven probe descriptions show in English, exactly as on German. The French and Italian for these fields do not exist yet; the drafts for the four model pages can be produced from the English source once the rules here are approved.

## 2. French: rules specific to Swiss French

| # | Rule | Instead of | Write |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Swiss punctuation.** No space before ? ! ; and a space before the colon. Guillemets « » with spaces inside. | "une démonstration ?" "Questions fréquentes ?" (French-France spacing throughout) | "une démonstration?" "Prix : sur offre" |
| F2 | **"offre", not "devis"** (rule 5). | obtenir un devis; devis personnalisés | demander une offre; nous établissons une offre pour chaque configuration |
| F3 | **"Suisse romande"** with a lower-case r; "Suisse alémanique". | Suisse Romande | Suisse romande |
| F4 | **Job titles without "&" and without doubled nouns.** | Responsable Échographie Suisse Romande & Responsable de la Stratégie | Responsable échographie Suisse romande et stratégie |
| F5 | **"échographe" for the machine, "échographie" for the exam, "sonde" for the probe.** Keep "appareil d'échographie" as a variant, never "système d'échographie" alone in body copy. | systèmes d'échographie (12×), imagerie échographique | échographes, échographie |
| F6 | **"haut de gamme" or "premium", not both.** | système haut de gamme (premium) | échographe haut de gamme |
| F7 | **"formation d'application" / "spécialiste d'application"** is the Swiss term and already used; keep it. Avoid "formation applicative" and "applications cliniques" side by side. | formation applicative (home), Responsable d'applications cliniques (team) | formation d'application, Responsable des applications |
| F8 | **Verbs over nominal chains.** | "pour une évaluation clinique avancée", "garantissant ainsi l'accès à ce qui se fait de mieux" | "pour évaluer le foie", "vous avez accès aux systèmes actuels" |
| F9 | **Check-list in article titles is fine in Swiss French; "liste de contrôle" in body copy.** | | |
| F10 | **Titles: "Échographes Samsung en Suisse" pattern**, as the Italian hub already has. | Solutions d'échographie en Suisse \| Meditron | Échographes Samsung en Suisse : distributeur officiel \| Meditron |

## 3. Italian: rules specific to Swiss Italian

| # | Rule | Instead of | Write |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | **"offerta", not "preventivo"** (rule 5). | richiedere un preventivo | richiedere un'offerta |
| I2 | **"cassa malati" / "assicurazione malattie"**, never "assicurazione sanitaria" or Italian institutions (ASL, SSN). Article 9 says "assicuratori malattia"; align to "cassa malati". | assicuratori malattia | cassa malati (colloquial) or assicurazione malattie (official) |
| I3 | **"ecografia", not "ultrasuoni", for the medical use.** "Ultrasuoni" is the physics. | Responsabile Ultrasuoni Romandia, Specialista Applicativo Ultrasuoni, Consulente Tecnico-Scientifico Ultrasuoni | Responsabile ecografia Svizzera romanda, Specialista applicativo ecografia, Consulente ecografia |
| I4 | **"Svizzera romanda", "Svizzera tedesca", "Ticino"; never "Romandia" or "SUI".** | nella regione della Romandia; in SUI e Francia | nella Svizzera romanda; in Svizzera e in Francia |
| I5 | **"Meditron Svizzera" in titles, not "Meditron Switzerland".** Every IT product title ends in English. | SAMSUNG HERA Z20 – Ecografia \| Meditron Switzerland | Samsung HERA Z20 – ecografo \| Meditron Svizzera |
| I6 | **"studio medico" for a practice, "ospedale", "clinica".** Avoid "struttura" as the default. | la vostra struttura | il vostro studio, il vostro ospedale |
| I7 | **"assistenza" and "servizio tecnico", not "supporto".** | supporto locale, supporto post-vendita, fornisce supporto | assistenza in Svizzera, servizio tecnico |
| I8 | **Voi form consistently**; the site already uses "Desiderate", "vostra". Never "tu" and never "Lei" in body copy. | "nella sua lingua" (hub, Lei form) | "nella vostra lingua" |
| I9 | **Verbs over nominal chains**; cut "al contempo", "in totale sicurezza", "ad alte prestazioni". | "consentendo così ai medici di prendere decisioni informate in totale sicurezza" | "così i medici decidono con dati misurati, non stimati" |
| I10 | **Listings: "Scoprite", not "Sfogliate".** | Sfogliate gli ecografi Samsung | Scoprite gli ecografi Samsung |

## 6. How to apply the rules

1. **Approve the rule set** (sections 1–3), one reviewer per language: Henri or Nikita for German, a Romandie colleague for French, a Ticino or Italian-speaking colleague for Italian. Comments on the Google Doc or on this file.
2. **Update the glossary** (docs/glossary.md) with the approved terms: offre/offerta/Offerte; assistance/assistenza/Service; gynécologie-obstétrique/ginecologia e ostetricia/Gynäkologie und Geburtshilfe; cassa malati; Suisse romande/Svizzera romanda; model articles.
3. **Rewrite the English source first** where the English itself is the problem (the old product descriptions are English marketing copy; the 20 October rewrites of Z20, V8, R20, V7 already follow rule 1 and 10). Translations are only as good as the source.
4. **Re-localise the four model pages and the hub** from the new English with the rule set in the prompt, then the FAQ rows and the team page. Send the result per language for sign-off before it goes live.
5. **Fix the two site-wide defects in Framer**: plain-text headings instead of the letter animation, and the FR/IT (and DE) values for the fields that fall back to English.
6. **Checklist for every new page**, in the SOP: no adjective from rule 1; "gynécologie-obstétrique" not "santé des femmes"; one quote word; one model article; Swiss punctuation in French; "Meditron Svizzera" in Italian titles; facts in the Why Meditron block; numbers formatted per language.
