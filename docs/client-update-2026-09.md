# Meditron SEO — status & next 2 months
*3 September 2026 · for the client*

## Goal
Rank in Swiss Google for **"Samsung ultrasound"** in all four languages — *samsung ultraschall* (DE), *échographe samsung* (FR), *ecografo samsung* (IT), *samsung ultrasound* (EN) — plus the Samsung model names (HS40, V8, HERA W10, HS60, HM70 EVO, R20) and the buying terms around them (price, used, leasing). Nobody in Switzerland ranks for these today; the results are Samsung's own pages and German/French/Spanish dealers. The difficulty scores are low (0–12 of 100), so with the right pages this is winnable inside the programme.

## What we found
- The new website is technically clean but currently ranks for only **1 keyword** in Swiss Google. The other Swiss Samsung distributor ranks for 87.
- Meditron has a **stronger link profile** than that competitor (154 linking websites vs 111) — but every link to an old‑site page now lands on a "Page Not Found" error, so that authority is being wasted.
- There are **no German/French/Italian versions** of the site yet; the competitor has all three.
- Google sometimes confuses *Meditron* with *Medtronic* on the brand search.

## Working on right now (weeks 1–2)
1. **Redirect map** — every old‑site URL that still carries links or traffic is being mapped to the right new page and pushed to Framer by script. This is the single fastest win and goes first.
2. **Measurement in place** — Google Search Console, Analytics and weekly rank tracking for ~60 keywords across the four languages, so every Monday report shows movement against a baseline.
3. **Language setup in Framer** — DE/FR/IT locales with translated URLs and correct hreflang tags, so Google serves the right language to the right searcher.
4. **Brand search** — homepage title, both office listings on Google (Google Business Profile), Knowledge Panel, so "Meditron" returns Meditron.
5. **Baseline technical audit** of the new site (speed, indexing, structured data).
