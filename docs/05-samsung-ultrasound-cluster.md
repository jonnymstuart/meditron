## What this changes in the plan
1. **The hub is the brand hub, not a generic ultrasound hub.** URL and H1 carry the phrase in each language:
   `/samsung-ultrasound/` · `/de/samsung-ultraschall/` · `/fr/echographe-samsung/` · `/it/ecografo-samsung/` (Framer translated slugs). Title pattern: *Samsung Ultraschall Schweiz – offizieller Distributor | Meditron* (the "Schweiz/Suisse/Svizzera" stays in the title for local trust, just not as the target phrase).
2. **Model pages sit under it** with brand+model slugs (`/de/samsung-ultraschall/hera-w10/`, `/fr/echographe-samsung/v8/`…). HS40 is the surprise volume leader in DE; V8, HERA W10, HS60, HM70 EVO, R20 next.
3. **Price/used pages** in IT and DE (`ecografo samsung prezzo/usato`, `samsung ultraschall preis`) — "request a quote" + financing/leasing + certified pre‑owned, no numbers unless the client supplies them.
4. **Generic category terms** (ultraschallgerät, échographe, ecografo, ultraschall) stay in the tracked set as tier 1 *secondary* targets — they are the bigger prize and the brand hub + model pages naturally rank for them, but the client's KPI line is the brand phrase.
5. **Tracking set** for the weekly report = the 4 head phrases + their inversions + 6 model terms × 4 languages + 4 price/used terms (≈ 60 keywords). `data/keywords/seed-ch.csv` updated.
