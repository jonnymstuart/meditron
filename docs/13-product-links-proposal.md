# Meditron – Samsung V8, R20 and V7: proposed Compatible Products, Variants and Related Products
_Proposal, 27 October 2026. Nothing has been changed on the site. The HERA Z20 is already set up this way on staging and is the reference. The probe lists come from Samsung's published product pages; Meditron confirms the Swiss configuration before anything goes live. No prices anywhere._

## How the three link types are used
| Section on the product page | What goes in it | Rule for Samsung systems |
|---|---|---|
| Compatible Products | Add-ons that work with this system. Each card opens the add-on's own product page. | The probes (transducers) Meditron offers for the system. One probe page can sit under several systems. |
| Variants | Versions of the same product you can order: a picture, a name, one line. No page of their own, not clickable. | Orderable configuration options of the system itself, such as monitor size. Leave empty if Meditron prefers to handle options in the quote; the section then hides itself. |
| Related Products (shown as "Related models") | Other systems a buyer should also look at. | The two or three neighbouring Samsung models: one step up, one step down, one for a different specialty. |
