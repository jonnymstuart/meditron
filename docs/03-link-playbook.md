# Link playbook (Swiss medical B2B — quality over volume)

Reality check: no reputable tool builds backlinks automatically. What automates well is **prospecting, drafting and follow‑up**; the send and the relationship stay human (assistant). Target: 10–20 new relevant referring domains in 5 months. Meditron already has 154 — recovering them via redirects comes first.

## Order of attack
1. **Reclaim** — every legacy URL with backlinks → 301 to the best new page (`/redirect-audit`). Ask the 5–10 biggest linking domains to update the link (Semrush `backlinks` report).
2. **Partner/manufacturer** — Samsung Healthcare "where to buy"/partner pages (EU + global), Samsung Medison distributor list, other represented brands (Clarius, RTI, Sun Nuclear, etc.) partner pages.
3. **Associations & events** — SGUM/SSUM (Schweizerische Gesellschaft für Ultraschall in der Medizin) sponsor/industry pages, Swiss Medtech, SGGG/SSGO, SGK cardiology, Swiss Congress of Radiology, Ultraschall‑Kurse listings (Zürich, Bern, Lausanne, Geneva, Lugano course pages link to equipment sponsors).
4. **Directories that matter** — medicalexpo.com (already lists Samsung Medison), Kompass CH, local.ch / search.ch business, Swiss Medtech member directory, Google Business Profile ×2 offices, LinkedIn, Wikipedia (Samsung Medison distributors — only if it fits).
5. **Content‑earned** — case studies with Swiss clinics (they link back), course listings, a yearly "ultrasound in Swiss practice" buyer guide in DE/FR pitched to practice‑management publications (Medinside, Medical Tribune CH, Revue Médicale Suisse business pages), university hospital news on installations.
6. **Digital PR (light)** — new‑model launch (HERA/V‑series) press note in DE/FR to Swiss medtech media; RIMED 5×V8 delivery is exactly this.

## Workflow
Semrush `backlinks_refdomains` on smdmedical.ch, praxisdienst.com, and 3 EU Samsung distributors → Claude scores/dedups → Airtable "Outreach" base → Claude drafts DE/FR/EN emails from templates → assistant sends from her/his own mailbox (or Postaga in months 3–4) → weekly status in report.

## Never
Paid links, link exchanges pages, PBNs, mass directory submission tools, comment/forum spam, "guaranteed DA 50 backlinks" sellers.
