## Description

The Samsung V8 is the high-end system in Samsung's V series: one platform for general imaging, women's health, musculoskeletal and vascular work. It is the system Meditron recommends when a practice or department needs premium image quality across several specialties rather than a dedicated OB/GYN or radiology console.

The V8 is built on Samsung's Crystal Architecture: CrystalBeam beamforming, CrystalLive image processing and S-Vue single-crystal transducers. On top of that sit the Intelligent Assist tools that cut exam time, such as ViewAssist for automatic labelling of anatomy, NerveTrack for locating nerves during live scanning and UterineAssist for uterine measurements. S-Shearwave Imaging quantifies tissue stiffness for breast and liver; MV-Flow shows slow microvascular flow; S-Fusion aligns live ultrasound with MRI or CT.

Meditron has supplied Samsung ultrasound systems in Switzerland since 2012. We configure the V8 for your mix of examinations, install it, train your team and service it from Rolle and Frauenfeld in German, French and Italian.

## FAQ

### Is the Samsung V8 a general imaging or a women's health system?
Both. Samsung positions the V8 for general imaging and women's health, and it is the model Meditron recommends when one system must cover OB/GYN, abdominal, MSK and vascular examinations. A practice that scans women's health all day is usually better served by the HERA Z20.

### What is the difference between the V8 and the V7?
Both use Samsung's Crystal Architecture. The V8 is the higher model with the larger monitor option, the full set of Intelligent Assist tools and the premium transducer range. The V7 is the choice when the budget is tighter and the focus is abdominal, liver and routine general imaging.

### Which transducers come with the V8?
The V8 uses Samsung's S-Vue single-crystal transducers: convex, volume convex, endocavity, linear and phased-array probes. Meditron configures the set for your specialty; the compatible list on this page is being completed with the Swiss configuration.
