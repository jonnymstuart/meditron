## Description

The Samsung V7 sits below the V8 in Samsung's V series and shares its Crystal Architecture: CrystalBeam beamforming, CrystalLive image processing and S-Vue single-crystal transducers. It is the system for practices and departments that want high-end image quality and the liver and tissue-stiffness tools without the full V8 configuration.

Its strengths are quantification: S-Shearwave Imaging for breast and liver stiffness, EzHRI, TAI and TSI for liver fat assessment in real time, and S-Flow for peripheral vessels. The V7 covers abdominal, general imaging, MSK, vascular and women's health examinations on one console with a 21.5" monitor, upgradeable to 23.8" or 27" OLED.

Meditron has supplied Samsung ultrasound systems in Switzerland since 2012. We configure the V7 for your examinations, install it, train your team and service it from Rolle and Frauenfeld in German, French and Italian.

## FAQ

### What is the Samsung V7 used for?
General imaging with a focus on abdominal and liver work, plus MSK, vascular and women's health. The liver quantification tools (EzHRI, TAI, TSI, S-Shearwave) are the reason many practices choose it.

### V7 or V8?
Both share the Crystal Architecture. The V8 adds the full Intelligent Assist set, the premium transducer range and the larger monitor as standard. The V7 is the right choice when the budget is tighter and the focus is abdominal, liver and routine general imaging.

### Can the V7 monitor be upgraded?
Yes. The standard 21.5" monitor can be replaced by a 23.8" LCD or a 27" OLED.
