## Description

The HERA Z20 is Samsung's premium ultrasound system for gynaecology and obstetrics. It is built for practices and clinics that scan women's health all day: fertility work-ups, first-trimester screening, detailed fetal anatomy, fetal heart, and gynaecological and breast exams on the same system.

It is the top of Samsung's women's health range in Switzerland. Compared with the V8, which Meditron recommends when one system has to cover general imaging and OB/GYN, the HERA Z20 adds Samsung's second-generation Crystal Architecture, a 27-inch OLED monitor and the full set of automated OB/GYN tools: Live ViewAssist, BiometryAssist, HeartAssist and 5D CNS+. Compared with the earlier HERA W10, it is the newer platform with the current AI workflow.

Meditron has installed Samsung ultrasound systems in Swiss practices and hospitals since 2012. We configure the HERA Z20 for your specialty, install it, train your team on it, and service it from Rolle and Frauenfeld in German, French and Italian.

## Highlights

- Crystal Architecture 2nd Gen: Samsung's current imaging engine, with software beamforming for penetration and detail in difficult patients.
- Live ViewAssist identifies the standard views while you scan, annotates up to 47 structures and performs up to 46 measurements without user interaction.
- BiometryAssist and HeartAssist: one-click fetal biometry and automated fetal heart measurements, for reproducible results between operators.
- 5D CNS+ locates nine planes from a fetal brain volume and produces six measurements automatically.
- MV-Flow and LumiFlow show slow, microvascular flow; ShadowHDR and RealisticVue improve visibility and give lifelike fetal rendering for parents.
- 27" OLED monitor and a 15.6" tilting touchscreen, lockable wheels, wide control-panel range and lower fan noise for long scanning days.

## FAQ

### How much does the Samsung HERA Z20 cost in Switzerland?
Price depends on the transducers and software packages you choose, so Meditron quotes each configuration individually, with leasing and trade-in options. Ask for a quote and we come back within two working days. Our article on Samsung ultrasound prices in Switzerland explains what drives the price.

### HERA Z20 or Samsung V8: which one for my practice?
Choose the HERA Z20 if the system will mainly do gynaecology and obstetrics and you want the automated OB/GYN tools (Live ViewAssist, BiometryAssist, HeartAssist, 5D CNS+) and the 27" OLED monitor. Choose the V8 if one system has to cover general imaging, abdomen, vascular and MSK as well as OB/GYN. Our comparison article goes through both in detail.

### HERA Z20 or HERA W10?
The HERA Z20 is the newer platform. It runs Samsung's second-generation Crystal Architecture and the current AI workflow (Live ViewAssist, 5D CNS+, UterineContour). The HERA W10 remains a strong women's health system and Meditron services it; for a new purchase in Switzerland we recommend the Z20. If you own a W10, ask us about trade-in.

### Which transducers come with the HERA Z20, and can I lease it?
The HERA Z20 has four active ports and accepts Samsung's current S-Vue transducers: convex, volume convex, endocavity, volume endocavity and linear probes (see the list on this page). A typical OB/GYN configuration uses a convex, a volume convex and an endocavity probe. Yes, leasing is available through Meditron's financing partners, and we also offer certified pre-owned Samsung systems.
