## Description

The Samsung R20 is the premium radiology and general imaging system in Samsung's range. It is built for hospitals and imaging centres that need one console from screening to intervention: abdominal, liver, breast, thyroid, vascular, MSK and contrast studies, with the AI tools to make the measurements reproducible.

The R20 carries Samsung's current image technologies: HQ-Vision to correct signal distortion in tissue, ShadowHDR to suppress shadows, A-Focus for sharpness across the field without manual focal zones, and MV-Flow for slow microvascular flow. For the liver it offers quantitative ultrasound (TAI and TSI) and Deep USFF fat-fraction measurement; Live LiverAssist and Live BreastAssist guide the standard views. The 27" OLED monitor and 15.6" tilting touchscreen are the same as on the HERA Z20.

Meditron has supplied Samsung ultrasound systems in Switzerland since 2012. We configure the R20 for your department, install it, train the team and service it from Rolle and Frauenfeld in German, French and Italian.

## FAQ

### What is the Samsung R20 used for?
General imaging and radiology: abdominal and liver studies, breast and thyroid, vascular, MSK, contrast-enhanced ultrasound and ultrasound-guided intervention. It is Samsung's top console for that work; the HERA Z20 is the equivalent for women's health.

### What are the liver tools on the R20?
Quantitative ultrasound with TAI and TSI, Deep USFF fat-fraction measurement, S-Shearwave Imaging for stiffness and Live LiverAssist for the standard views. Together they give a non-invasive, repeatable liver assessment on one system.

### R20 or V8?
The V8 is the all-round high-end system for practices that mix general imaging and women's health. The R20 is the radiology console with the larger monitor, the full liver and contrast toolset and the premium transducer range. Meditron will show you both on your own cases.

### Does Meditron handle PACS integration and training?
Yes. DICOM and worklist integration, installation, applications training and service are all done by Meditron in Switzerland.
