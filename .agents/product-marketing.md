# Product Marketing Context — Meditron (meditron.ch)
Document version: v1

## Company
Meditron SA, Switzerland, founded 1991. Distributor and service partner for complex medical equipment: ultrasound (official Samsung Healthcare distributor for 12+ years), radiotherapy, radiology QA, radiation detection/dosimetry. Offices in German‑ and French‑speaking Switzerland. Tagline on site: "Treat With Precision".

## Product in scope for this programme
Samsung Healthcare diagnostic ultrasound systems sold, installed, trained and serviced in Switzerland: premium (HERA W10 / W9 / Z20, V8, R20), mid‑range (V7, V6, HS60, HS50, HS40), portable/point‑of‑care (HM70 EVO, PT60A), plus probes, training, service & repair, financing/leasing, certified pre‑owned. Out of scope: radiotherapy/QA/dosimetry lines.

## Target audience (ICP)
Swiss buyers of diagnostic ultrasound: gynaecology/obstetrics practices, radiology and general‑imaging departments, cardiology, hospitals (procurement + department heads), GP/group practices, MSK/orthopaedics, physiotherapy, veterinary clinics. Languages: German (≈65% of CH), French, Italian (Ticino), English (international staff/expat clinics). Decision drivers: image quality, workflow, local service response time, training, total cost/financing, Swissmedic/CE compliance.

## Positioning
"The official Samsung ultrasound partner in Switzerland — 30 years of local service, training and support behind every system." Differentiators vs. the other Swiss Samsung reseller (SMD Medical) and EU dealers: exclusivity/officiality, longevity, two‑region presence, application specialists, demo & training, Swiss‑based service.

## Competitors
smdmedical.ch (Samsung + Siemens, Swiss), praxisdienst.com, EU Samsung dealers ranking in DE/FR/IT SERPs (sonocheck.de, isonic.fr, tmamedica.com), GE/Philips/Canon/Mindray distributors in CH.
