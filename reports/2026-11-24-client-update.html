Meditron website: results and progress, 24 November 2026

Prepared by Rise for Meditron SA. Sources: Google Search Console (web search, read 24 November), DataForSEO (Swiss Google and AI assistants, measured 24 November).

Summary

  • Visits from Google almost doubled: 425 clicks in the last four weeks, against 220 in the four weeks before.
  • meditron.ch now appears in Google two and a half times as often: 10,800 times in the last four weeks, against 4,300.
  • Most of the growth comes from people searching for products and topics, not for the name Meditron.
  • Google has not yet read most of the pages we improved in mid-November. We asked it to on 24 November, so the full effect will show in the 1 December reading.

AI assistants

We ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the same 20 questions in four languages (60 answers) about buying a Samsung ultrasound system in Switzerland. Compared with 10 November:

  • meditron.ch cited as a source: 27 of 60 answers (from 23).
  • Meditron named in the answer: 13 of 60 (from 18).
  • Meditron named first: 9 of 20 questions (from 10).

AI answers vary from one day to the next. More citations means the AI tools are reading our pages; we expect the number of times Meditron is named to follow once the updated pages are read again.

What we did since 10 November

  • New Google titles and descriptions for all solution and product pages, in four languages.
  • Samsung ultrasound page: 10 frequently asked questions, a comparison of the seven Samsung models, a guide on which model suits which speciality, and new, leasing and pre-owned options, in a new two-column layout.
  • The four other solution pages received the same layout with an introduction, reasons to choose Meditron and a product comparison.
  • Samsung model pages: clear headings, six questions each, and links to the matching guides.
  • The product page error that Google reported on every product page is fixed. Google confirmed the fix on 24 November and is now re-checking all product pages.
  • Three new articles: handheld ultrasound with Clarius, veterinary ultrasound with Esaote, and Samsung probes explained.
  • Phone and tablet display fixed on product pages and on the Samsung page.
  • Length limits in the product editor, so product texts edited by your team stay within what Google shows.
  • On 24 November we asked Google to read the Samsung page and the Samsung model pages again. The remaining pages follow over the next days, as Google accepts about ten requests a day.

What comes next

  • Samsung page: show the Samsung introduction and model comparison first, with the other brands further down.
  • Product pages: a clear "Request an offer" button, and labelled phone and email contacts.
  • Strengthen the V8 page and the French pages for "échographe samsung".
  • Next measurement on 1 December.

What we need from you

  3. Fonts: the website uses trial versions of the Graphik and Denim fonts. Please check whether Meditron holds full licences.
  4. Names, photos and regions of your application specialists, and 2 or 3 customers who would give a short quote.
