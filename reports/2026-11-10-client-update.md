# Meditron website – update for the week of 3 to 10 November 2026

Dear Meditron team,

This week was about the languages. All Samsung ultrasound pages on www.meditron.ch now read as if written in Switzerland, in German, French and Italian. Below is what changed, with examples, so your reviewers know what to look for.

## 1. German, rewritten for Switzerland (live)

Covers the HERA Z20, V8, R20, V7, V4 to V6, the seven probe pages, the Samsung overview page, 25 FAQ entries and the nine articles.

- "Ultraschallsystem" is now "Ultraschallgerät" throughout.
- "ein detailliertes schriftliches Angebot" is now "eine detaillierte schriftliche Offerte".
- "Neu oder gebraucht" and "Gebrauchtmarkt" are now "Occasion" and "Occasionsmarkt".
- "Support" is now "Service"; "Ende des Supports" is now "Ende der Software-Updates".
- "Krankenhaus" is now "Spital"; "Frauengesundheit" is now "Gynäkologie und Geburtshilfe".
- "Workflow" is now "Arbeitsablauf"; ß is written ss; "1.000" is written "1'000".
- Article titles were sharpened, for example "Welches Samsung Ultraschallgerät passt zu meiner Praxis?"
- Three FAQ answers to "Wer ist Meditron?" were still in English. They now read "Meditron SA ist seit 2012 der offizielle Distributor von Samsung Healthcare Ultraschall in der Schweiz …"

## 2. French, rewritten for Suisse romande (live)

The French model pages had been automatic translations. They are now proper Swiss French.

- Swiss punctuation: no space before ? ! and ;
- "un devis" is now "une offre".
- "système d'échographie" is now "échographe"; "L'HERA Z20" is now "Le HERA Z20".
- "flux de travail" is now "protocole d'examen".
- "support local" is now "service en Suisse"; "support logiciel" is now "mises à jour logicielles".
- "santé des femmes" is now "gynécologie-obstétrique".
- "formation applicative" is now "formation d'application".

## 3. Italian, rewritten (live)

Several Italian model and probe pages had been showing English. They are now in Italian.

- "supporto" is now "assistenza"; "salute della donna" is now "ginecologia e ostetricia".
- "flusso di lavoro" is now "percorso d'esame"; "agli assicuratori malattia" is now "alla cassa malati".
- "nella sua lingua" is now "nella vostra lingua"; "1.000" is written "1'000".
- Page titles end with "| Meditron Svizzera".

## 4. Corrections after the 5 November publish

- That publish brought back an older version of the German and French product texts. We restored the new texts the same day.
- Two page addresses that Framer had translated automatically (for example /de/ultraschall) were put back to their original form, so no links break. Automatic translation is now switched off in all languages.
- The unfinished "solution configurator" section was hidden.

## 5. Also done this week

- Google Analytics is now installed on every page, so we can see visits and which pages people land on.
- Page titles and descriptions for all 212 products in four languages were completed. Example: "SAMSUNG V8 – Ecografia | Meditron Switzerland" became "Samsung V8 – Ultraschall | Meditron Schweiz".
- A technical error that Google Search Console reported on every product page (a price field with no price) was removed.
- Probe page titles that read "Samsung Samsung …" in French and Italian were corrected.
- Six of the twelve articles now show real Samsung product photos instead of illustrations.
- Articles 10 to 12 are live.
