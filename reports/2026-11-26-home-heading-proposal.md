# Homepage heading and description: proposal for Meditron (26 November 2026)

For Meditron to approve or change. Nothing below is live yet.

## Why
- The homepage heading is "Treat with Precision", split over two headings, in English on every language version.
- Google reads the main heading to understand what a page is about. Today it says nothing about ultrasound, Samsung or Switzerland.
- The description in Google says "Seit über 12 Jahren" / "for 12+ years". Meditron has been the Samsung distributor since 2012, so it is now 14 years, and the text would need updating every year.

## Proposal
The slogan "Treat with Precision" stays on the page as a line of text. Only the heading changes.

| Language | Heading | Description in Google (≤155 characters) |
|---|---|---|
| DE | Samsung Ultraschall und Medizintechnik für die Schweiz | Offizieller Samsung Ultraschall-Distributor der Schweiz seit 2012: Ultraschallgeräte, Radiologie und Strahlentherapie, mit Service in der ganzen Schweiz. |
| FR | Échographes Samsung et technologies médicales pour la Suisse | Distributeur officiel des échographes Samsung en Suisse depuis 2012 : échographie, radiologie et radiothérapie, avec service dans toute la Suisse. |
| IT | Ecografi Samsung e tecnologie medicali per la Svizzera | Distributore ufficiale degli ecografi Samsung in Svizzera dal 2012: ecografia, radiologia e radioterapia, con assistenza in tutta la Svizzera. |
| EN | Samsung ultrasound and medical technology for Switzerland | Official Samsung ultrasound distributor in Switzerland since 2012: ultrasound, radiology and radiotherapy systems, with service across Switzerland. |

## Questions for Meditron
1. Is the wording right, and does "Service in der ganzen Schweiz" match what you offer?
2. Should the slogan stay in English ("Treat with Precision") or be translated?
