Dear Meditron team,

Here is where the website stands: results so far, what we changed since our last update, what comes next, and four things we need from you.

## Results

Google Search Console, 7 September to 16 November 2026 (since the new website went live):

- 685 visits from Google search and 12,800 appearances in Google results.
- 5.3% of people who see meditron.ch in Google click on it.
- Average position in Google: 10.7.
- The trend is up. In September the site appeared in Google about 50 to 100 times a day. Since early November it is 300 to 600 times a day, about five times more. Daily visits roughly doubled over the same period.

Rankings in Swiss Google for the four target searches (DataForSEO, 17 November, best of three searches; 10 November in brackets):

- "samsung ultraschall": position 6 (18)
- "samsung ultrasound": position 8 (1)
- "échographe samsung": position 8 (7)
- "ecografo samsung": not in the top 50 (29)

Positions are moving a lot this week. The same search returned position 6 once and 53 twice within an hour. That is normal for a few weeks after a large change to a website, and we published many changes on 16 November. Google has not yet re-read most of the updated pages, so we will judge the effect on the 24 November reading, once the pages are indexed.

AI answers (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, 10 November): Meditron is named in 18 of 60 answers and cited as a source in 23, up from 20 in October.

## What we did since 10 November (all live)

Samsung ultrasound page (www.meditron.ch/solutions/ultrasound and the DE, FR and IT versions):
- New Google titles in each language, for example "Samsung Ultraschall Schweiz – Distributor | Meditron" and "Échographe Samsung en Suisse | Meditron".
- 10 frequently asked questions (official distributor, offer, pre-owned, leasing, service and warranty, training, which model for which speciality, delivery and installation).
- A table comparing the seven Samsung models.
- The FAQ answers are now in the page code itself, so Google and AI tools such as ChatGPT can read them without clicking.

Samsung model pages (HERA Z20, V8, R20, V7, V6, V5, V4):
- Clear page headings, for example "Samsung V8 ultrasound system" instead of "SAMSUNG V8".
- New Google titles and descriptions in all four languages.
- 6 FAQs per model and links to the matching guides.

All 212 product pages:
- Google titles shortened to 60 characters and descriptions to 155, so Google no longer cuts them off.
- Search Console flagged an error on every product page ("price missing"). The pages now describe the products as medical devices without a price, which removes the error. Google confirms the fix over the next 1 to 2 weeks as it revisits the pages.
- The "Technical Data" table now reads left to right, with room for longer descriptions.
- 258 German product texts used "ß"; they now use the Swiss "ss".

Making the product pages hard to break:
- In Framer, each product field now shows its length limit in its name, for example "Meta Title (max 60)" and "Meta Description (120–155)". The five fields Google relies on are now required.
- We added a check that lists any product that breaks the rules (too long, missing translation, duplicate title, price in the text, and so on). We run it before every publish. We can send your product editors a one-page guide.

Also: Google Analytics is on every page, the sitemap was checked, the overlapping "Guides" boxes on the Samsung page were fixed, and the "Let's talk" contact block no longer animates.
